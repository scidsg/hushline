Hush Line can receive ordinary connection details when this page and its files load, including that the workspace page was requested. Your notes stay in this page's memory and are not sent to Hush Line while you create, edit, or delete them.

Notes are readable by this page and your browser while it is open. This is not encrypted or saved draft storage.
